### Questions for the seller

#### Unit and price

1. What is the final price and what does it include (furniture, taxes, commission)?
2. Please clarify "Currency" and send a document that confirms it.
3. What is the unit area on the tapu (gross/net)?
4. Please clarify "Room type" and send a document that confirms it.
5. Which floor is the unit on?
6. What is the view from this specific unit?
7. What is the claimed yield based on? Are there statements of actual payouts to owners for past years?

#### Project

1. What is the exact address and plot number (ada/parsel)?
2. Please send the coordinates or a map link with the exact location of the building.
3. Is this a hotel, apart-hotel or serviced residence according to its documents?
4. Does the hotel operate under a chain brand, and under what agreement (franchise, management)?
5. Which legal entity operates the hotel? Please send its name and tax number.
6. Who is selling the unit: the developer, the operator or a private owner via an agency?
7. Is the hotel operating or under construction? If under construction, what completion date is in the contract?
8. What star rating is on the KTB licence?
9. Does the hotel operate year-round or seasonally? In which months is it closed?

#### Rental

1. Is there a rental programme? Please send a sample contract.
2. Is it a rental pool or individual letting of the unit?
3. What share of rental income goes to the owner and on what base is it calculated?
4. Who, as a party to the contract, guarantees the income, for how long, and what happens if the operator changes?
5. How many nights a year may the owner stay in the unit and are there restrictions by month?
6. For what term is the rental/management agreement signed, how can it be terminated and what happens if the operator changes?

#### Title and documents

1. Is a separate tapu issued for the unit? Please send a copy with the plot number.
