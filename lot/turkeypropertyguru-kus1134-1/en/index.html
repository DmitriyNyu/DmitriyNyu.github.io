Questions for the seller

Unit and price

  1. What is the price of this specific unit (type, area) and in which currency is it fixed in the contract?
  2. What is the gross and net area of the unit per the title deed / project?
  3. Please clarify "Room type" and send a document that confirms it.
  4. On which floor is the unit?
  5. What is the view from this specific unit?
  6. What yield do you claim for this unit and what is it based on (the operator's actual reports for past years)?

Project

  1. Which part of the complex is this unit in: the seasonal hotel or Lagoon?
  2. Is this a hotel, apart-hotel or serviced residence according to its documents?
  3. Who is the hotel operator (legal entity) and who is the counterparty of the owner's rental agreement? What happens if the operator changes?
  4. Who is the developer (legal entity) and who is the seller under the sale contract?
  5. Is the property operating or under construction? If under construction — the contractual completion date and building permit (yapı ruhsatı); if operating — the opening year and occupancy permit (iskân).
  6. What official star rating has the hotel been assigned (per the Ministry of Culture and Tourism certificate)?
  7. How many rooms are in the hotel part in total and how many of them are sold to private owners?
  8. Please confirm with a management-company document: is the main hotel closed from November to 15 March, and does Lagoon operate all year?
  9. Please send the management company’s income calculation.
  10. Please send an invoice for the monthly fee.
  11. Please clarify "The monthly fee is deducted from the income" and send a document that confirms it.
  12. Please clarify "Personal outgoings in the rental" and send a document that confirms it.
  13. Please send the management company’s calculation of this payment.
  14. Please clarify "Payments in pounds" and send a document that confirms it.
  15. Please clarify "Income is paid twice a year" and send a document that confirms it.
  16. Please clarify "Personal use" and send a document that confirms it.
  17. Please clarify "Notice of personal use" and send a document that confirms it.
  18. Please clarify "Operating season — per the seller’s letter" and send a document that confirms it.
  19. Is this the complex the hotel calls its own golf facility?
  20. Which figure is the shore and which is the beach?

Rental

  1. Is there a rental programme run by the operator? Please send a sample agreement.
  2. Is the rental programme a shared pool (income split among all owners) or individual (income from your own unit)?
  3. What share of rental income does the owner receive and which costs are deducted before it is calculated?
  4. Is there a guaranteed income? Who exactly guarantees it (legal entity), for how long, on what base amount, and how is the guarantee secured?
  5. How many nights a year may the owner stay in the unit and are there restrictions by month?
  6. For what term is the rental/management agreement signed, how can it be terminated and what happens if the operator changes?
  7. What is the management commission and what other costs are deducted from the owner's income?
  8. How can the owner leave the rental programme or sell the unit: notice period, penalties, who finds the buyer?

Title and documents

  1. Is a separate title deed (kat mülkiyeti / kat irtifakı) issued for the unit? Please send a copy showing the plot number.
  2. Please give the full legal name (unvan), tax number (vergi no) or MERSIS of the developer and the hotel-owning company; have they had konkordato, liquidation, a change of owners or icra sales involving the property?
