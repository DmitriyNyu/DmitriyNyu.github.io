### Questions for the seller

#### Project

1. Please give the exact address of the project (il / ilçe / mahalle, street) and the land registry plot (ada / parsel).
2. Is this a hotel, apart-hotel or serviced residence according to its documents?
3. Under which hotel brand does (will) the property operate? Is a franchise or management agreement signed — please send a copy or an extract.
4. Who is the hotel operator (legal entity) and who is the counterparty of the owner's rental agreement? What happens if the operator changes?
5. Who is the developer (legal entity) and who is the seller under the sale contract?
6. Is the property operating or under construction? If under construction — the contractual completion date and building permit (yapı ruhsatı); if operating — the opening year and occupancy permit (iskân).
7. What official star rating has the hotel been assigned (per the Ministry of Culture and Tourism certificate)?
8. Does the hotel hold a Ministry of Culture and Tourism licence (Turizm İşletme Belgesi)? Please send its number and a copy.
9. How many rooms are in the hotel part in total and how many of them are sold to private owners?
10. Does the hotel operate year-round or seasonally? In which months is it closed?

#### Rental

1. Is there a rental programme run by the operator? Please send a sample agreement.
2. Is the rental programme a shared pool (income split among all owners) or individual (income from your own unit)?
3. What share of rental income does the owner receive and which costs are deducted before it is calculated?
4. Is there a guaranteed income? Who exactly guarantees it (legal entity), for how long, on what base amount, and how is the guarantee secured?
5. How many nights a year may the owner stay in the unit and are there restrictions by month?
6. For what term is the rental/management agreement signed, how can it be terminated and what happens if the operator changes?

#### Title and documents

1. Is a separate title deed (kat mülkiyeti / kat irtifakı) issued for the unit? Please send a copy showing the plot number.
2. Please give the full legal name (unvan), tax number (vergi no) or MERSIS of the developer and the hotel-owning company; have they had konkordato, liquidation, a change of owners or icra sales involving the property?
